Transparent QR Code Generator

Create a transparent QR code from any URL — perfect for business cards.

Keeping transparency when sharing: email and chat apps (Messenger, WhatsApp…) often convert inline photos to JPEG and lose transparency. Send the file as an attachment/document — not as a photo — or use the SVG export for design tools.

The checkered area shows where the background is transparent.

Free and open source. Everything is generated in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
